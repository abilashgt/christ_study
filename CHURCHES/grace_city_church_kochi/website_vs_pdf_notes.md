# Grace City Church Kochi — Website vs PDF Notes

## Sources

- PDF: `DOC-20240525-WA0001.pdf`, Grace City Church Membership Class Handout, dated 25 May 2024
- [What we believe](https://www.gracecitychurch.in/what-we-believe)
- [Who we are](https://www.gracecitychurch.in/who-we-are)

## Main observation

The website is a public summary of Grace City Church's identity and core beliefs. The PDF is an internal membership handout containing the fuller statement of faith, membership expectations, church covenant, and church constitution.

There is no major doctrinal contradiction between the two sources. The PDF is more precise and reveals several practical positions that the website does not state prominently.

## Comparison notes

| Topic | Website | PDF | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

| Humanity | Fallen, spiritually dead and under condemnation | Adds image of God, equal dignity and worth, human purpose and accountability | PDF presents both dignity and depravity more explicitly |

| Holy Spirit | Included in the God section | Regeneration, illumination, indwelling, assurance, sanctification and empowerment | PDF provides substantially more detail |
| Salvation | Grace, faith, regeneration and holy living | Adds justification, imputed righteousness, adoption, repentance, faith and perseverance | PDF is more explicitly Reformed |
| Election | Sovereignty is emphasized elsewhere on the website | God calls sinners and grants repentance and faith | Election remains under-explained in both sources |

| Dispute resolution | Not clearly addressed | Members are expected to resolve disputes through biblical processes rather than secular courts | Significant policy found only in the PDF |

## Practical conclusion

The website is sufficient to understand Grace City Church’s broad theological identity: evangelical, Reformed, Baptist, congregational and Great Commission-oriented.

The PDF is necessary to understand the implications of membership. It clarifies baptism by immersion, male eldership, women serving as deacons, church discipline, membership obligations, congregational voting, civil government, and internal dispute resolution.

For evaluating the church, the most important questions not answered clearly in either source concern:

- marriage and sexual ethics;
- spiritual gifts and charismatic practice;
- creation and origins;
- the detailed position on election;
- safeguards and exceptions surrounding church discipline and internal dispute resolution;
- whether this 2024 PDF remains the current governing document.
