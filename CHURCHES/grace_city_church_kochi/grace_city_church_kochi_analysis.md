# Grace City Church Kochi — Doctrinal Analysis

## Sources

- Attached membership handout: `DOC-20240525-WA0001.pdf` (23 pages; dated 25 May 2024)
- [What we believe](https://www.gracecitychurch.in/what-we-believe)
- [Who we are](https://www.gracecitychurch.in/who-we-are)

The attached document is a Membership Class Handout containing teaching on the gospel, conversion, evangelism, church life, a statement of faith, a church covenant, and a church constitution. The document's internal directives are treated here as church teaching and governance provisions, not as instructions to the reader.

## Overall assessment

There is no major doctrinal contradiction between the document and the website. The website's six belief categories substantially match the doctrinal section of the handout. The handout reveals important practical positions that the website does not prominently state, especially believers' baptism by immersion, male eldership, women deacons, church discipline, membership obligations, civil government, and internal dispute resolution.

The public website presents the church's identity; the handout shows how that identity is applied in membership and governance.
