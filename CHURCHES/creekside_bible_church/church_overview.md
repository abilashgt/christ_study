## Why It Belongs In The South Bay List

Creekside is a South Bay church in Cupertino with a strong emphasis on biblically rooted preaching, teaching, counseling, discipleship, and pastoral training.

## Master's Seminary / MacArthur-World Connection

Creekside has a meaningful Master's Seminary-world connection through Pastor Cliff McManis and Creekside's pastoral training ministry.

Creekside's official pastoral training page says Pastor Cliff teaches at Irpin Biblical Seminary in Kyiv, Ukraine, and that Irpin "was started in the early 1990s under the leadership of John MacArthur and The Master's Seminary."

## Pastor Cliff McManis

- Role: Pastor-Teacher at Creekside Bible Church
- Since 2006, Creekside says Pastor Cliff has taught apologetics, theology, and other classes at The Cornerstone Seminary in Vallejo, California.
- Creekside says he serves on The Cornerstone Seminary Board of Directors.
- Creekside says he is a visiting professor at Novosibirsk Biblical Theological Seminary in Siberia.
- Creekside says he teaches Apologetics and Bible Exposition at Irpin Biblical Seminary in Kyiv, Ukraine.

## Summary

If you are looking for a South Bay church that is not confessional Presbyterian/Reformed but is still serious about exposition, doctrine, and pastoral training, Creekside is one of the stronger options.
