# Armenian Christian Denominations

The main Armenian Christian traditions are:

1. **Armenian Apostolic Church** — the historic national church of the Armenian people. It belongs to the **Oriental Orthodox** family, not the Roman Catholic or Eastern Orthodox churches. It has two major seats:
   - **Mother See of Holy Etchmiadzin** in Armenia
   - **Catholicosate of the Great House of Cilicia** in Antelias, Lebanon

2. **Armenian Catholic Church** — an Eastern Catholic church in communion with the Pope of Rome.

3. **Armenian Evangelical Church** — a Protestant tradition, alongside other evangelical communities such as Baptists and Pentecostals.

The Armenian Apostolic Church is the largest Armenian Christian tradition and is the dominant church in Armenia.

## Comparison

| Tradition | Christian family | Main authority / leadership | Relationship to Rome | Distinctive features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Armenian Apostolic Church** | Oriental Orthodox | Catholicos of All Armenians at Etchmiadzin; Catholicosate of Cilicia also serves its own jurisdictions | Not in communion with Rome | Ancient Armenian national church; traditional Armenian liturgy and sacraments |
| **Armenian Catholic Church** | Eastern Catholic | Armenian Catholic Patriarch-Catholicos | In communion with the Pope | Armenian liturgy and heritage combined with Catholic doctrine and worldwide Catholic communion |
| **Armenian Evangelical Church** | Protestant / Evangelical | Pastor- and church-led structures | Not in communion with Rome | Emphasis on Scripture, preaching, personal faith, and Protestant theology |

*Etchmiadzin and Cilicia are two jurisdictions within the Armenian Apostolic Church, not two separate denominations.*

## Doctrinal Differences

| Topic                         | Armenian Apostolic                                                                                                                | Armenian Catholic                                                                                        | Armenian Evangelical                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Christology**               | Miaphysite Christology; accepts the first three ecumenical councils and does not accept the Council of Chalcedon as authoritative | Accepts Chalcedon and the doctrinal authority of the Catholic Church while preserving the Armenian rite  | Generally accepts the historic Protestant and wider Christian confession of Christ’s full divinity and humanity; details vary by church                           |
| **Final doctrinal authority** | Scripture, apostolic tradition, church fathers, and the church’s bishops and councils                                             | Scripture and Sacred Tradition, interpreted under the teaching authority of the Pope and bishops         | The Bible is the final authority for faith and practice                                                                                                           |
| **Sacraments / ordinances**   | Seven sacraments, including baptism, chrismation, confession, and Eucharist                                                       | Seven sacraments                                                                                         | Usually two ordinances: baptism and the Lord’s Supper                                                                                                             |
| **Baptism**                   | Normally includes infants and is followed by chrismation                                                                          | Normally includes infants                                                                                | Often emphasizes believer’s baptism, though practice varies among Armenian Evangelical churches                                                                   |
| **Eucharist / Lord’s Supper** | Sacramental Eucharist with the real presence of Christ                                                                            | Sacramental Eucharist with the real presence of Christ                                                   | Views vary, but it is generally understood as an ordinance commemorating Christ rather than as the same sacramental system as the Apostolic and Catholic churches |
| **Salvation**                 | Salvation is by God’s grace and is lived out through faith, repentance, baptism, the sacraments, and life in the church           | Salvation is by God’s grace through faith, expressed through the sacraments and a life of faith and love | Generally emphasizes justification by grace through faith in Christ and the necessity of personal conversion                                                      |
| **Mary and the saints**       | Mary and the saints are honored; believers may ask for their intercession                                                         | Mary and the saints are honored and may intercede for believers                                          | Worship and prayer are directed to God through Christ; veneration and invocation of saints are generally rejected                                                 |

*This is a broad comparison. Armenian Evangelical beliefs and practices can differ between Baptist, Reformed, Pentecostal, and independent congregations.*
