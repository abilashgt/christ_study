# Reformed Churches in India

## Search criteria

- Grace City Church is included as the reference church; comparison churches are outside Kochi
- Clearly evangelical, Reformed, or Baptist
- Publishes a clear doctrinal statement or confession on its own website
- No public identification as Pentecostal, charismatic, Spirit-filled, full-gospel, or apostolic
