# Reformed Churches in India

## Search criteria

- Grace City Church is included as the reference church; comparison churches are outside Kochi
- Clearly evangelical, Reformed, or Baptist
- Publishes a clear doctrinal statement or confession on its own website
- Explicit Pentecostal, charismatic, Spirit-filled, full-gospel, or apostolic identities remain excluded; sign-gift positions are recorded separately when clearly documented

### Redemption Hill statement comparison

- **Current statement:** Redemption Hill describes itself as a conservative, confessionally Reformed Presbyterian church that subscribes to the Westminster Standards, is led by elders, and practices expository preaching. [Current beliefs](https://redemptionhill.in/beliefs/) | [Leadership](https://redemptionhill.in/officers/)
- **Older statement:** An official affirmation still hosted on the church’s domain says that signs and wonders continue and that all biblical spiritual gifts continue to be eagerly desired and exercised. [Older official affirmation](https://redemptionhill.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Church-Affirmation-of-Faith.pdf)
- **Assessment:** Included in the main table because it is clearly Reformed Presbyterian and not explicitly Pentecostal or Spirit-filled; the sign-gifts difference is recorded in its own column.

## Excluded from this list

Churches were excluded when their public website was vague about doctrine or identified them with Pentecostal, charismatic, Spirit-filled, full-gospel, or apostolic theology.

- [New City Church Mumbai](https://www.newcitymumbai.com/) — Mumbai, Maharashtra: Baptist? Not formally identified as Baptist, although it was planted out of Bombay Baptist Church. Its official beliefs affirm baptism and fullness of the Holy Spirit, the continuing exercise of spiritual gifts, and a five-fold ministry including apostles and prophets. Pastor/lead planter: Anand Mahadevan; assistant pastor: Felix Carey Pakki. Excluded because it does not meet the non-Pentecostal/non-charismatic filter. [Official beliefs and team](https://www.newcitymumbai.com/looking-for-a-church/) | [Parent network](https://www.newcitymumbai.com/about/)
