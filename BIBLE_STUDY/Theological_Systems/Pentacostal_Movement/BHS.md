# Baptism of the Holy Spirit (BHS)

`BHS` means **baptism of the Holy Spirit**.

## Basic Meaning

In Christian theology, this phrase refers to the Holy Spirit coming upon or indwelling a believer in a special way. Different traditions define it differently.

## Major Views

### Pentecostal / Charismatic View

- BHS is often understood as a distinct experience after conversion.
- It is associated with empowerment for witness, worship, and spiritual gifts.
- Commonly linked with tongues, prophecy, healing, and boldness in ministry.

### Baptist / Reformed / Many Evangelical Views

- BHS is usually understood as happening at conversion.
- It marks the believer’s union with Christ and entrance into the body of Christ.
- It is not treated as a second, separate event for all Christians.

## Key Texts

- Acts 1:5
- Acts 2
- 1 Corinthians 12:13

## Summary

In plain terms, BHS refers to receiving the Holy Spirit in a way that marks and empowers Christian life. The main disagreement is whether it happens at conversion or as a later distinct experience.
