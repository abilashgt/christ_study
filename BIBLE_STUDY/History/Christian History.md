# Christian History

| Period | Approx. Date | Church Figures | Outside Church | Key Events | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Apostolic Church | 30-100 AD | Jesus, Peter, Paul, John, James | Roman authorities, Jewish leaders | Jesus' death, resurrection, ascension, Pentecost, missionary expansion, New Testament writings | Christianity begins as a Jewish movement centered on Jesus as Messiah |
| Early Church | 100-313 AD | Ignatius, Polycarp, Irenaeus, Tertullian, Origen | Roman emperors, local governors | Growth of bishops, church fathers, persecution, defense of doctrine | Major theological debates develop on Christ, God, and salvation |
| Imperial Christianity | 313-500 AD | Athanasius, Basil, Gregory of Nyssa, Augustine | Constantine, Theodosius I, Roman imperial officials | Edict of Milan, Council of Nicaea, Council of Constantinople, Roman support | Trinity and creedal orthodoxy are formalized |
| Medieval Church | 500-1500 AD | Gregory the Great, Anselm, Thomas Aquinas, Francis of Assisi, John Wycliffe | Charlemagne, feudal rulers, crusader kings | Rise of papacy, monasteries, scholasticism, crusades, schisms | Western and Eastern churches continue to diverge |
| Reformation | 1500-1700 AD | Martin Luther, John Calvin, Huldrych Zwingli, John Knox | Henry VIII, Charles V, Elizabeth I | Luther, Calvin, Zwingli, Anglican break from Rome | Protestants emphasize Scripture, faith, and reform |
| Post-Reformation | 1700-1900 AD | John Wesley, George Whitefield, William Carey, Charles Finney, Hudson Taylor | British Empire, American founders, colonial administrators | Revival movements, missions, denominational growth | Christianity spreads globally through evangelism and colonization |
| Modern Era | 1900-Present | Billy Graham, Aimee Semple McPherson, Karl Barth, C. S. Lewis, Pope John Paul II | World War leaders, secular governments, media figures | Pentecostalism, ecumenical movement, global Christianity, digital theology | Christianity becomes increasingly worldwide and diverse |

## Major Turning Points

- 30-33 AD: Death and resurrection of Jesus.
- 1st century: Spread of the gospel and writing of the New Testament.
- 325 AD: Council of Nicaea.
- 1054 AD: Great Schism between East and West.
- 1517 AD: Protestant Reformation begins.
- 19th-20th centuries: Global missionary expansion and denominational multiplication.
