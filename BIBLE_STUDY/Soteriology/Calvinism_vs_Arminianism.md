# Calvinism vs. Arminianism

Calvinism and Arminianism are two major Protestant approaches to understanding salvation. Both affirm salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, but they differ especially in how divine sovereignty and human responsibility relate.

| Topic | Calvinism | Arminianism | Commonly cited Bible verses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human condition | Humans are spiritually unable to turn to God without regenerating grace. | Humans are unable to turn to God without grace, but God gives prevenient grace that enables a genuine response. | **Calvinist:** Ephesians 2:1–5; Romans 8:7–8; 1 Corinthians 2:14<br>**Arminian:** John 12:32; Titus 2:11; Acts 7:51 |
| Election | Unconditional: God chooses whom to save according to His will, not based on foreseen faith. | Conditional: God elects those He foreknows will believe in Christ. | **Calvinist:** Ephesians 1:4–5; Romans 9:10–18; John 6:37, 44<br>**Arminian:** Romans 8:29; 1 Peter 1:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:3–4 |
| Atonement | Often described as limited or definite: Christ died savingly for the elect. | General or unlimited: Christ died for all, though only believers receive salvation. | **Calvinist:** John 10:11, 14–15; Matthew 1:21; Ephesians 5:25<br>**Arminian:** John 3:16; 1 Timothy 2:4–6; 1 John 2:2; Hebrews 2:9 |
| Grace | Irresistible or effectual grace successfully brings the elect to faith. | Grace can be resisted; people may reject God’s invitation. | **Calvinist:** John 6:37, 44; Acts 13:48; Romans 8:30<br>**Arminian:** Matthew 23:37; Acts 7:51; Hebrews 3:12–13 |
| Conversion | Regeneration precedes and produces faith. | Faith precedes regeneration; people respond to God’s enabling grace. | **Calvinist:** John 3:3–8; Ephesians 2:4–5; 1 John 5:1<br>**Arminian:** Acts 16:31; Romans 10:9–13; Ephesians 1:13 |
| Perseverance | Those truly saved will persevere to the end. | Many Arminians believe a believer can fall away from faith. | **Calvinist:** John 10:27–29; Philippians 1:6; Romans 8:38–39<br>**Arminian:** Hebrews 6:4–6; Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22 |
| Primary emphasis | God’s sovereignty and initiative in salvation. | Human responsibility and the universal offer of salvation. | **Calvinist:** Romans 9:18–23; Ephesians 1:11<br>**Arminian:** Deuteronomy 30:19; Joshua 24:15; Revelation 22:17 |
| Common proponents | John Calvin, Jonathan Edwards, Charles Spurgeon. | Jacob Arminius, John Wesley, many Methodists and Pentecostals. | — |

## Calvinist summary: TULIP

- **T**otal depravity
- **U**nconditional election
- **L**imited atonement
- **I**rresistible grace
- **P**erseverance of the saints

These labels summarize broad tendencies; individual Calvinists and Arminians may qualify or explain some points differently. The verses above are representative passages commonly used in each tradition’s interpretation, not proof-texts accepted in only one tradition.
