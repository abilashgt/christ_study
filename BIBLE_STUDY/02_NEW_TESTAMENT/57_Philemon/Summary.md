## Background

### Philemon
- Paul met Philemon at Ephesians. Probably saved at the same time (v19)
- Rich Christian with a large house church
- Owns slaves
- Philemon was one of only 3 individuals (Timothy and Titus) receive Paul's letter.
### Onesimus
- Onesimus means "useful" in Greek
- Runaway slave of Philemon
	- While not a believer
	- Stole money and ran away (v18)
- Onesimus met Paul in Rome.
- Onesimus got saved by Paul (v10)
- Paul send him back to Colosse with Tychicus (Col 4:7-9)
