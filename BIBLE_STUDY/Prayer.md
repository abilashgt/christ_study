



## When God does not listen 

“For what is the hope of the **godless** when he is cut off,
When God requires his soul?
Will God hear his cry
When distress comes upon him?”
— Job 27:8-9

“There they cry out, but He does not answer
Because of the **pride** of evil men.
Surely God will not listen to an empty cry,
Nor will the Almighty perceive it.”
— Job 35:12-13

“If I see **wickedness in my heart**,
The Lord will not hear;”
— Psalm 66:18

“When he is **judged**, let him come forth a **wicked** man,
And let his **prayer become sin**.”
— Psalm 109:7

“Yahweh is far from the **wicked**,
But He hears the prayer of the righteous.”
— Proverbs 15:29

“He who turns away his ear from **listening to the law**,
Even his prayer is an **abomination**.”
— Proverbs 28:9

“So when you spread out your hands in prayer,
I will hide My eyes from you;
Indeed, even though you multiply prayers,
I will not listen.
Your **hands are full of blood**.”
— Isaiah 1:15

We know that God does not listen to **sinners**; but if anyone is God-fearing and does His will, He listens to him.
— John 9:31

“You husbands in the same way, live with your wives in an **understanding way**, as with a weaker vessel, since she is a woman; and show her honor as a fellow heir of the grace of life, so that your prayers will not be **hindered**.”
— 1 Peter 3:7
