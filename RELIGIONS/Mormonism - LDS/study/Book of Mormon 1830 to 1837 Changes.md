# Book of Mormon 1830 to 1837 Changes

The 1837 Kirtland edition was a revision of the 1830 first edition. It was based on the printer's manuscript and included editorial emendations and grammatical corrections.

## Main Change Types

- Spelling standardization
- Capitalization cleanup
- Punctuation changes
- Grammar fixes
- Occasional wording clarifications

## What Stayed The Same

- Overall narrative
- Book order
- Witness statements
- Title-page material
- Core doctrinal content

## Examples Of Wording Corrections

- `counsel the Lord` became `Counsel with the Lord` in Alma 37:37
- `but if he yieldeth` became `unless he yieldeth` in Mosiah 3:19

## Scope

The 1830 to 1837 difference is large at the line level, but most changes are minor editorial fixes rather than substantive rewrites. A complete list requires a verse-by-verse textual collation.
