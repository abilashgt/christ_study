# Book of Mormon 1830 to 1837 Exact Changes

This note records exact wording changes that are commonly cited and verifiable.

## Exact Wording Changes

- **Alma 37:37**
  - 1830: `counsel the Lord in all thy doings, and he will direct thee for good;`
  - 1837: `Counsel with the Lord in all thy doings, and he will direct thee for good;`

- **Mosiah 3:19**
  - 1830: `but if he yieldeth to the enticings of the Holy Spirit, ...`
  - 1837: `unless he yieldeth to the enticings of the Holy Spirit, ...`

## Notes

- The 1837 edition also included many spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and grammar edits.
- A truly exhaustive 1830-to-1837 diff requires a full textual collation of the two editions.
- This file is intentionally limited to exact substantive wording changes that are easy to verify and cite.
