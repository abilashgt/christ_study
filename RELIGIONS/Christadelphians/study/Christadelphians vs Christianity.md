# Christadelphians vs Christianity

| Topic | Christadelphians | Mainstream Christianity |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| God | One God, the Father alone | Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit |
| Jesus | Son of God and Messiah; fully human | Divine Son of God, often understood as co-equal with the Father |
| Holy Spirit | God's power, not a separate person | Third person of the Trinity |
| Scripture | Bible alone as doctrinal authority | Bible as authority, often alongside creeds or tradition |
| Salvation | Faith, repentance, and baptism by immersion are essential | Salvation by grace through faith; baptism viewed differently across denominations |
| Death | No immortal soul; the dead are unconscious until resurrection | Varies, but most teach conscious existence after death |
| Hell | No eternal torment; final judgment ends in death | Often teaches eternal punishment, though views vary |
| Future hope | Return of Christ, resurrection, and God's kingdom on earth | Return of Christ, resurrection, and final judgment; details vary |
| Church structure | No central hierarchy; local ecclesias | Usually denominational hierarchies or formal church structures |

## Key Difference

Christadelphians are a non-trinitarian restorationist movement that rejects several doctrines common in historic creedal Christianity, especially the Trinity, the immortality of the soul, and eternal torment.
